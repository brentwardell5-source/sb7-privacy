SB7 Cardz Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026.

SB7 eBay Connection is a private seller tool operated by Brent Wardell for SB7 Cardz.

Information used

The connection stores the owner's ChatGPT account identifier, eBay developer credentials, seller authorization tokens, and seller account identifiers to secure the connection and access the owner's listings. Credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest. Live listing responses are read on request and are not retained. No buyer addresses or order data are requested.

Use and sharing

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, maintain the eBay connection, display seller listings, and process required eBay account deletion notifications. It is not sold or used for advertising. The service relies on OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare hosting and communicates with eBay APIs.

Retention and removal

The owner can remove stored connection credentials using the setup page. Valid eBay deletion notifications remove matching seller connection data. The owner may also revoke application access in eBay.
